## Common prompt

Create one standalone premium editorial pen-and-ink illustration, landscape 3:2 1536x1024, for Namaha Studios, an Indian film studio website. Upgrade from a simplistic outline into a beautifully crafted illustrated scene: confident varied-weight black ink contours, fine interior detail, selective delicate crosshatching and stippled shadows, believable anatomy, well-observed mechanical and architectural details, strong composition and layered depth. Refined hand-drawn travel-sketchbook meets cinema production illustration, elegant rather than cartoonish. Black ink with restrained muted ochre-gold accents only. Pure flat WHITE background, lots of breathing space around the scene, all subjects inside a central 80% safe area. Overall balanced medium detail and enough dark ink to read at website scale. No paper texture, no beige background, no vignette, no gradients, no photographic rendering, no border, no letters, no text, no logos or watermark.

Append `Scene: ` and the corresponding description below.
