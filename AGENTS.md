# Namaha Studios artwork direction

The user-approved style is a lightly detailed, hand-sketched line drawing. Use the current Home hero and `LINE_DRAWING_STANDARD.md` as the reference for every illustration across the site.

- Thin warm charcoal-brown contours, a modest amount of descriptive detail and generous open space.
- No painting, opaque colour washes, cartoon rendering, heavy dark strokes, shaded volumes or dense engraving.
- Suns and other coloured accents must use separated ochre/golden-orange pencil strokes with visible paper gaps. Never use a solid filled gold circle.
- Apply this direction to all page heroes, supporting story/service/process art, success/404 illustrations and the single kite—not just Home.
- Preserve supplied brand artwork and recognizable brand symbols. Keep photographic portfolio/gallery content photographic unless the user asks otherwise.
- Use the existing asset pipeline, inspect every final image and verify its actual desktop/mobile placement. When replacing cached website assets, provide fresh delivery URLs and check that the pages reference them.

Earlier detailed illustration prompts are historical and must not override this standard.

The 6 November client update uses the exact George Williams Monospace family from the supplied 1001 Fonts link, self-hosted with its license.
